Seed Garden

Not connected

Generative art that lives on NEAR. Each NFT gets a unique seed from on-chain randomness at mint, and the artwork, Harmonious donuts, is drawn live in p5.js from that seed. Same seed, same art, forever.

Works with NEAR wallets and Ethereum wallets like MetaMask; the NFT is always minted on NEAR. On this testnet demo, use Meteor Wallet (create a free testnet account at wallet.meteorwallet.app). OKX and MetaMask (NEP-518) are wired in but not yet working on testnet.
